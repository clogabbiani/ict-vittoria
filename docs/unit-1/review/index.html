Questions like the ones in the real test. Try them on paper first, then check.

  1. Label a PC: match each part to what it does.

    • power supply — ?
    • motherboard — ?
    • CPU — ?
    • RAM — ?
    • SSD — ?

  2. In a phone, the CPU and the GPU are inside one chip, the SoC.

    True or false?

  3. Put the six milestones in order, oldest first.

    • Babbage's Analytical Engine (1837)
    • ENIAC (1946)
    • the GPU (1990s)
    • the Intel 4004 (1971)
    • the mouse and windows (1980s)
    • the transistor (1947)

  4. Circuit X: two switches in series, one open and one closed. Is the bulb on, and which gate is it?

    1. on: OR
    2. off: AND
    3. on: AND

  5. Convert 01001000 to decimal.

  6. Write 13 as an 8-bit binary number.

  7. Which one forgets everything when the power goes?

    1. RAM, the workbench
    2. the SSD, the pantry
    3. the hard disk

  8. Why are chatbots trained on GPUs and not on CPUs?

    1. a GPU has thousands of simple cooks for millions of similar small sums at once
    2. a GPU has more storage
    3. a GPU uses no electricity

  9. System software or application?

    • Word — ?
    • the Wi-Fi driver — ?
    • Android — ?

  10. Your friend takes a photo: explain what happens until it appears on another screen, naming the components and one milestone that made each possible.
