Unit 1 · Extra

Extra: switches, binary and secret codes

Not in the test, but this is how the machine really works underneath: switches, ones and zeros, and the codes Turing broke.

Not part of the test. For anyone who wants more.

01Binary lights

Every transistor is a switch: on or off, 1 or 0. Eight switches make a byte, and a byte can count to 255.

02Letters as numbers

A computer stores only numbers, so letters are numbers too: the ASCII table says that 72 is H.

03Circuit bench

Two switches in series make an AND gate; two in parallel make an OR. Billions of these gates are the ALU.

04The Caesar wheel

More than 2,000 years ago Julius Caesar hid his messages by shifting each letter three places. Easy to use, easy to break: only 25 keys.

05Mini-Enigma

Enigma changed the key after every letter, which is why it needed a machine to break it. Try it.

06Moore's law chart

Every dot is one chip. Switch between the linear and the log scale to see why the log scale is used.
